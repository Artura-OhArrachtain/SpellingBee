PRIVACY

SpellingBee browser extension privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

What the extension does

SpellingBee reads text only after you open its popup on the active page. It sends that text to the SpellingBee app you configure, so the app can request proofreading suggestions from its configured AI provider.

Information handled

  • Selected text or text from the active editor is sent to your configured SpellingBee app when you choose Check writing.
  • Your configured app address is stored in the browser's local extension storage.
  • The extension does not collect browsing history, passwords, payment information, or advertising data.
  • The extension does not sell user data or use it for advertising.

Where text goes

The extension sends text to the app address you enter. That app may forward the text to the AI provider configured by its operator. Review the privacy terms of that app deployment and AI provider before sending sensitive text. Text is not sent until you choose Check writing.

Permissions

The extension uses active tab and scripting access to read and replace text in the page you are working in, storage to remember the app address, and optional access to the app origin you approve. It does not run a background content collector.
